# Package scope — DRAFT for Venkatesh's approval

Nothing in this file is shown on the website until you approve it.
To publish a package's limits, edit `data/pricing.json` and change that package's `"scope.status"` from `"draft"` to `"approved"`. The configurator and Ask Sociam then show the limits; until then they say "confirmed in a written scope before you pay".

These are proposals based on typical delivery effort for a fixed-price package. Change any line.

## Real Estate Website — $300

| Item | Proposed |
|---|---|
| Pages | Up to 5: Home, About, Projects or Listings, Services, Contact |
| Content | Up to 6 project or listing entries |
| Revisions | 2 rounds |
| Delivery | 10 working days from receiving all content |
| Domain | First-year registration of one .com or .in up to $15; renewals at cost; registered in the client's name |
| Hosting | 12 months hosting and SSL included; renewal quoted 30 days before expiry |
| Client provides | Logo, photographs, project details and RERA numbers, within 5 working days of kickoff |
| Counts as extra | Extra pages, listing or CRM integrations, multilingual sites, custom features or portals |

## Ecommerce Website — $500

| Item | Proposed |
|---|---|
| Pages | Home, Shop, Collection, Product, Cart and Checkout, About, Contact, Policies |
| Catalogue | Up to 25 products in up to 5 categories, entered from the client's spreadsheet |
| Revisions | 2 rounds |
| Delivery | 15 working days from receiving product data |
| Domain | As above |
| Hosting | 12 months hosting and SSL where Sociam hosts; hosted platforms such as Shopify bill their subscription to the client directly |
| Client provides | Product photographs, descriptions, prices, stock, policies, a payment gateway account |
| Counts as extra | More products, custom apps, subscriptions, marketplaces, ERP or inventory integrations |

## Decisions still open
1. Which ecommerce platform you deliver on by default (Shopify, WooCommerce or your own stack). This changes who pays for hosting.
2. Whether a $15 domain allowance is right for the GCC and UK (.ae, .co.uk cost more).
3. Website care: monthly price and limits, if you want a fixed public price instead of "quote".

## Commercial check (fill in before scaling paid ads)
For each package, estimate: delivery hours, contractor cost, domain + hosting cost, payment processing and currency fees, expected revisions and support. At $300, 10–12 hours of delivery leaves little margin once hosting and the domain are paid; keep the scope tight and treat the package as an entry point to care and growth services.
