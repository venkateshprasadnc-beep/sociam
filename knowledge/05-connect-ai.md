# Sociam Connect AI

## What it is
Sociam Connect AI is the intelligence layer of a Sociam growth system. Four jobs: qualification (asks budget, timeline, location, configuration and summarises the enquiry), routing and CRM (sends enquiries to the right person and records them with source), follow-up (reminders for the team and consent-based messages on approved channels), and sales intelligence (leads, meetings, proposals and outcomes by source). Every setup is scoped per project to the client's channels, CRM and sales process, and quoted after a conversation. Page: /sociam-connect-ai/

## Channels
Website chat and forms are available. WhatsApp Business, Instagram messages and Facebook Messenger require Meta approval and are implemented through official APIs or approved providers, each according to its own permissions and messaging rules. CRM integrations are scoped per project. Sociam does not automate public social comments or messages outside platform rules. Do not claim any specific CRM integration already exists.
