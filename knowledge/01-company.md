# Sociam company facts

## Who Sociam is
Sociam is a website design and business growth systems company based in Malleshwaram, Bengaluru 560003, India. It helps businesses establish a compelling digital presence and build systems for acquiring, converting and retaining customers. Brand line: "Systems that Scale. Intelligence that Compounds." Founder: Venkatesh (Venki), who leads every engagement personally. Contact email: venki@sociam.in. Website: https://sociam.in.

## Markets served
Primary market: real estate developers, builders, brokers and channel partners. Secondary market: ecommerce and D2C businesses. Other businesses can request a custom website or service quote. Sociam works with clients in India, the United States, the United Kingdom, Europe and GCC countries (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar and others).

## The growth flywheel
Sociam explains growth as a five-stage flywheel: 1. Foundation (brand, positioning, website). 2. Demand (search visibility, content, social media, lead generation). 3. Conversion (landing pages, qualification, follow-up, sales journeys). 4. Intelligence (analytics, AI, CRM, automation). 5. Compounding (retention, recurring services, referrals, optimisation). It is an explanatory model, not a promise of automatic or guaranteed growth.
