# Sociam services

## Website design and development
Three options: Real Estate Website (fixed starting price), Ecommerce Website (fixed starting price), and Custom Business Website (quoted to scope, for bespoke design, more pages or special features, and for businesses outside real estate and ecommerce). Every Sociam website includes clear positioning and copy, mobile-first UI/UX, basic SEO and GEO, enquiry capture with source recording, Google Business Profile integration where applicable, and testing before launch. Page: /website-design/

## Lead generation and conversion
Search and social campaigns aimed at people already looking; landing pages per project, product or offer; lead capture and qualification forms; reporting on leads, cost and outcome by source. Conversion optimisation reviews first-screen clarity, form length, speed and mobile usability, response time and first reply, follow-up from day one to day seven, and measurement. Advertising spend is paid by the client directly to the platform. No results are guaranteed. Quoted to scope. Page: /lead-generation/

## SEO, AEO and GEO
SEO: technical health, structure, local search and content for real customer questions. AEO (answer engine optimisation): content written to be quoted, such as FAQs, specifications and definitions. GEO (generative engine optimisation): consistent business information and evidence that AI assistants can cite. Monthly service, quoted to scope. Page: /lead-generation/#seo

## Social media management
Monthly planning, content creation in the brand's voice reviewed by the client, scheduled publishing and a short monthly report linking activity to visits and enquiries. Quoted by number of channels and posts. Advertising spend is separate. Page: /social-media-management/

## Brand strategy and brand management
Positioning, identity (name, logo use, colour, typography, tone of voice) and guidelines, quoted as a project. Brand management is an optional monthly service keeping website, social, brochures and sales decks consistent. Page: /brand-strategy/

## Website care
Optional monthly service: security and software updates, backups and uptime monitoring, small monthly content changes, hosting renewal where Sociam hosts, and a short monthly report where tracking exists. Limits, response times and renewal terms are set in writing; price is quoted per site. Page: /pricing/#care

## Optional extensions after launch
Website care, SEO/AEO/GEO, content and social media, brand management, lead generation, conversion optimisation, Sociam Connect AI, CRM and workflow automation, analytics and reporting. All optional; Sociam recommends a next step only when it solves a real problem. Nothing recurring starts without written agreement.
