# Process and policies

## How buying works
1. The visitor configures a website on /pricing/ or sends a project brief on /contact/. Nothing is charged. 2. Venkatesh replies within one working day. 3. The client receives a written scope covering pages, content, revisions, timeline, ownership and every third-party cost. 4. The client approves the scope and only then receives an invoice. 5. Work starts when the invoice is paid. Prices are invoiced in USD. No payment is taken on the website.

## Project process
Brief -> written scope -> design, copy and build (client reviews real pages with the agreed revision rounds) -> launch and handover (tested across devices, connected to Google, access handed over in the client's name).

## Ownership
The domain is registered in the client's name. Ownership and access are confirmed in the written scope and handed over at launch.

## Currency
Prices are set in USD. INR, GBP, EUR, SAR, AED, CAD and AUD amounts on the site are indicative conversions using recent exchange rates, not separate regional prices.

## Guarantees
Sociam does not guarantee rankings, leads, sales or revenue. Outcomes depend on factors outside Sociam's control.

## Privacy
Sociam uses enquiry details only to reply and prepare requested scopes or proposals, does not sell data, and does not add people to marketing lists without agreement. Details: /privacy/
