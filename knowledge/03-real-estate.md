# Real estate

## Who it is for
Developers and builders launching projects; brokers and channel partners presenting listings and handling enquiries. Page: /real-estate-websites/

## The real estate journey Sociam designs
Property discovery (search, maps, social, portals) -> project landing page (plans, price band, location, RERA details) -> lead capture (short forms, WhatsApp click-to-chat, source recorded) -> AI-assisted qualification (budget, timeline, configuration) -> sales-team handoff (right person notified with a summary) -> site-visit booking (visitor requests a slot, team confirms) -> follow-up (reminders and consent-based messages) -> booking outcome (won or lost with reason). Discovery, landing page and lead capture are part of the website package. Qualification, handoff, site-visit booking, follow-up and outcome tracking are scoped per project to the client's CRM, team and channels and confirmed in a proposal.

## Common real estate questions
RERA: project and agent RERA details are presented on the relevant pages using numbers the client provides. Portal and CRM integrations are scoped and quoted separately. A site-visit request form is straightforward; live calendar booking connected to the team is scoped per project. Sociam works with developers outside India; prices are in USD.
