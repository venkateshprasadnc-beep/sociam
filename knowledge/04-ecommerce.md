# Ecommerce

## What Sociam designs for
Product discovery (categories, search, filters), clear product information (sizes, materials, delivery, returns near the price), trust (policies, secure checkout, familiar payment options), a short checkout, analytics to find drop-off, and after-purchase updates. Page: /ecommerce-websites/

## Ecommerce costs to know
Payment gateway and transaction fees are paid by the client directly. Hosted platform subscriptions such as Shopify and paid apps are billed to the client by the platform. Marketplace, ERP or inventory integrations are additional work, quoted separately.
