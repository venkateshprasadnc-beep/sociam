Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026

This policy describes how the website is built to handle data. Have it reviewed by a qualified adviser for the countries you serve before launch.

Who we are

Sociam, Malleshwaram, Bengaluru 560003, India. Contact: venki@sociam.in.

What we collect

  • Details you send us through the configurator, project brief or Ask Sociam: typically your name, email, business name, phone or WhatsApp number, country, the services you're interested in, budget and timeline ranges, and anything you write in the message.
  • How you found us: the page you arrived on, the referring website and campaign tags (such as utm_source), attached to your enquiry.
  • Ask Sociam conversations: your questions and the assistant's answers are processed to generate replies. If you choose to send a brief from the chat, a summary and the recent conversation are attached to your enquiry.
  • Anonymous usage counts: page views and button clicks are counted per day without cookies, IP addresses or any identifier that could recognise you.

How we use it

Only to reply to your enquiry, prepare a scope or proposal you asked for, and keep a record of our conversation. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not add you to marketing lists without your agreement.

Who processes it

  • Netlify (website hosting and secure storage of enquiries)
  • Resend (delivery of enquiry notifications to Sociam)
  • Anthropic (generating Ask Sociam replies; conversations are not used by Sociam for anything other than replying to you)
  • ExchangeRate-API (exchange rates only; no personal data is sent)

Cookies

The website does not set tracking or advertising cookies. It uses your browser's session storage to remember your chat and how you arrived during a visit, and local storage to remember your preferred currency. Both stay on your device.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for as long as needed to respond and to keep a record of our business relationship, and for no longer than 24 months after our last contact unless you become a client.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or ask us to stop contacting you, by writing to venki@sociam.in. We'll act on it promptly.
