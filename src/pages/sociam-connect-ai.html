What it does

Four jobs, each scoped to your team.

Every Connect AI setup is designed around your channels, your CRM and how your team sells. The tags show what depends on that scoping.

Qualification

Asks for budget, timeline, location and configuration in a natural way, then summarises the enquiry for your team.

Scoped per project

Routing and CRM

Sends each enquiry to the right person and records it in your CRM, with source and summary attached.

Scoped per project

Follow-up

Reminds your team when a lead needs attention, and sends consent-based messages on approved channels.

Scoped per project

Sales intelligence

Shows leads, meetings, proposals and outcomes by source, so decisions are based on measured results.

Scoped per project
