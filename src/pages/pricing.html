What website care can cover

  • Security and software updates
  • Backups and uptime monitoring
  • Small content changes each month
  • Hosting renewal, where Sociam hosts
  • A short monthly report where tracking exists

Monthly limits, response times and renewal terms are set out in writing before care begins. Price is quoted to your site.

Not included in starting prices

  • Domain renewals after the first year
  • Payment gateway and transaction fees
  • Hosted platform subscriptions (for example Shopify) and paid apps
  • Advertising spend on any channel
  • Paid stock photography, fonts or plugins you request
  • WhatsApp Business API and messaging fees, where used
  • Extra pages, integrations or features beyond the agreed scope

How buying works

  1. You configure or send a brief. Nothing is charged.
  2. Venkatesh replies within one working day.
  3. You receive a written scope: pages, content, revisions, timeline, ownership and every third-party cost.
  4. You approve it, and only then receive an invoice.
  5. Work starts when the invoice is paid. Prices are invoiced in USD.

Questions

Pricing questions, answered plainly.

Do I pay on the website?
No. Sociam does not take payment on the website. You receive a written scope with the confirmed price, and an invoice only after you approve it.
Why are prices in USD?
Sociam works with clients in several countries and sets package prices in US dollars. Other currencies are shown as indicative conversions using recent exchange rates; your invoice is in USD.
Is the {{price:re-website}} website a template?
It starts from a Sociam real estate design system and is tailored to your business: your copy, your projects, your brand. Fully bespoke design is available as a custom website, quoted separately.
Who owns the domain and website?
The domain is registered in your name. Ownership and access are confirmed in your written scope and handed over at launch.
Can I start with a website and add growth services later?
Yes. Most clients start with a website. We suggest a next step only when it solves a real problem, and nothing recurring starts without your written agreement.
What if I need something the packages don't cover?
Choose "Custom business website" in the configurator or send a project brief. You'll get a scoped quote rather than a guess.
