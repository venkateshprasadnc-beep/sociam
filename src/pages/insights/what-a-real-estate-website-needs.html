What a real estate website needs before it can generate enquiries

Sociam5 minute read

By the time a buyer calls, they have usually decided whether they trust you. That decision happens quietly, on a phone, in a few minutes. These are the six things they check.

1. Are you real?

A proper domain, an office address, a consistent name across Google and social, and registration details that are easy to find. None of this impresses on its own. Its absence is noticed immediately.

2. Is the project information complete?

Location, configurations, carpet areas, price band, possession date and RERA number. Buyers who can't find these leave to look for them elsewhere.

3. Can I see it properly?

Real photographs, floor plans that open on a phone and a map with nearby landmarks. Renders are expected; honest photographs build trust.

4. Do you know this market?

Specific localities, specific projects, specific prices. General claims about "premium living" say nothing; a well-presented listing says a great deal.

5. Is it easy to ask a question?

A short form and a WhatsApp button on every page. Long forms that ask for everything up front lose buyers who were only ready to ask one question.

6. Will you be easy to deal with?

A quick, useful reply tells a buyer what working with you will feel like. That impression often matters as much as the property.
