Your product page does more selling than your ads

Sociam4 minute read

Ads bring people to the door. The product page decides whether they walk in. Yet many stores spend far more time refining campaigns than refining the one page every paying customer actually sees.

Answer the question before it's asked

Size, fit, ingredients, delivery time, returns. When these are hard to find, people leave to look for them and often don't come back. Put them close to the price and the button.

Photographs are the product

Online, the photograph is the only version of the product a customer can hold. Show scale, texture and use. One honest image in natural light does more than several polished renders.

Make the last step effortless

Every extra field at checkout is a small reason to stop. Fewer fields, familiar payment options and a clear delivery date remove most of them.

Measure before you redesign

Look at where customers drop off: product page, cart or checkout. Fix the biggest leak first, measure for two weeks, then move to the next.
